HLD vs LLD

This was the easiest way for me to understand the difference.

Think about building a house.

HLD

First, I decide:

        🏠 HOUSE
           |
    ┌──────┼──────┐
    ↓      ↓      ↓
 Living  Kitchen  Bedroom

I know the major parts of the system.

That's HLD.

LLD

Now I go inside those parts:

Kitchen
   |
   ├── Stove
   ├── Fridge
   ├── Sink
   └── Cabinets

And then I think about how these things are actually arranged and connected.

That's closer to LLD.

In software:
HLD
 ↓
Services
Databases
APIs
Architecture
Communication

        ↓

LLD
 ↓
Classes
Objects
Interfaces
Methods
Relationships
Design Patterns

So:

HLD = Big picture
LLD = Detailed structure

A simple software example

Suppose we're building a food delivery application.

At HLD level, I might have:

User
Restaurant
Order
Payment
Delivery
Notification

That's enough to understand the major parts of the system.

But if I start designing the Order part in LLD, I need to think deeper:

Order
 ├── orderId
 ├── user
 ├── items
 ├── totalAmount
 └── status

Methods:
 ├── addItem()
 ├── removeItem()
 ├── calculateTotal()
 └── cancelOrder()

Now we're actually thinking about objects, data, behavior and responsibilities.

That's where LLD starts.
