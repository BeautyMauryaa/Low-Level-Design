What makes a good LLD?

For me, a good design should have:

Clear responsibilities

Every class should have a clear reason to exist.

Low unnecessary coupling

Classes shouldn't depend heavily on the internal details of other classes.

High cohesion

Related responsibilities should stay together.

Easy extension

Adding a new feature shouldn't require rewriting half the system.

Reusability

Common behavior should be reusable where it makes sense.

Maintainability

Another developer should be able to understand the design without performing archaeological excavation. 💀
