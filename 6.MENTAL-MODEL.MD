My current mental model

This is how I'm currently looking at LLD:

             PROBLEM
                ↓
       Understand requirements
                ↓
        Identify objects
                ↓
      Give them responsibilities
                ↓
       Define relationships
                ↓
      Define their interactions
                ↓
      Apply OOP / SOLID / Patterns
                ↓
             CODE

The important part is that code comes after the thinking.

Not necessarily after hours of designing — just enough thinking to avoid creating a badly structured system.

HLD vs LLD — Quick Comparison
HLD	LLD
Big picture	Detailed design
System architecture	Code/object structure
Services	Classes
Databases	Objects
APIs & communication	Methods & interfaces
Scalability	Responsibilities & relationships
System-level decisions	Object-level decisions
One line to remember

HLD tells me what the major parts of the system are and how they communicate. LLD tells me how those parts are structured internally at the code/object level.
