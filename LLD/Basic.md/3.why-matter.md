Why does LLD matter?

Imagine I create one giant class:

ParkingSystem

And inside it I put:

findSpot()
parkVehicle()
generateTicket()
calculatePrice()
processPayment()
sendNotification()
...

It may work initially.

But later:

New vehicle type?
New pricing rule?
New payment method?
Different parking strategy?

Suddenly changing one thing can affect five other things.

That's a sign that the design wasn't thought through properly.

A good LLD should make changes easier, not harder.
