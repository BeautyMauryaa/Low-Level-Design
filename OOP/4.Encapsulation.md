# Encapsulation:

- wrapping data and behavior together
- restricting direct access to the object's internal data.
- controlling how the data is accessed or changed.

Take a bank account.

I don't want other parts of my application to directly do this:

```text
account.balance = -50000;
```

Instead, the account itself should control how its balance changes.

```text
BankAccount
-------------------
private balance

deposit()
withdraw()
getBalance()
```

So:

```text
account.deposit(1000);
account.withdraw(500);
```

The internal state is controlled by the object.

### Why is this useful in LLD?

Because it prevents random classes from modifying another object's internal state.

The class becomes responsible for protecting its own data.

### Simple way I remember it:

> **"Don't let everyone touch your internal stuff directly."**
